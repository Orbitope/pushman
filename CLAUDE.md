# Context Management
- ALWAYS read PLAN.md at the start of every session and after any /compact or /clear event. Section 0 ("Implementation Status") is the source of truth for what is done and what is next.
- Before starting any task, confirm the target item is listed as ❌ NOT STARTED or partially complete in Section 0.
- When a task is finished, update Section 0 of PLAN.md immediately — flip ❌ to ✅, add any new sub-items discovered, and note stubs or deferred work. Do not wait until the end of a session.
- After updating PLAN.md, notify the user what was marked complete and what the next pending item is. Wait for the user to confirm before starting the next task.
